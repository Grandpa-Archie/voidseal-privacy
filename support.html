Shadow Seals: Ninja Awakening — Support

Need help? Email shadowseals.support@gmail.com.

Please include your device model, iOS version, game version and what happened. Screenshots can help. Do not send passwords or full payment information.

Purchases

Open the in-game Shop to use Restore Purchases for eligible non-consumable purchases. Consumable gems and scrolls cannot be restored after use. For Apple billing or refund requests, visit Apple's purchase support.

Saved progress

Progress is stored on your device. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting: we do not keep a server copy of your save.

Sound and reminders

Use Settings & more in the game to change music, effects, haptics and reminders.
