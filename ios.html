Privacy Policy — Shadow Seals: Ninja Awakening for iOS

Developer: Voidseal Games · Last updated: 14 October 2026

Game data

The iOS game has no player accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or tracking. Game progress, characters, currencies and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a game server or receive your local save automatically.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit to verify transactions and grant purchases, and stores transaction identifiers and entitlements locally to avoid granting purchases twice. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Reminders

Optional reminders are scheduled locally on your device after permission is granted. You can disable them in the game or iOS Settings. No push-notification server is used.

Diagnostics and support

The game does not automatically send gameplay diagnostics to us. Local diagnostic files may be created during support testing and remain on the device unless you choose to share them. If you email support, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate your request, and retain them only as needed for support or legal obligations. Do not send passwords or payment card details. You may request deletion of support correspondence using the address below. Apple may separately provide platform diagnostics according to your Apple settings and its privacy policy.

Deleting data

Deleting the app removes its local data. We do not maintain a server copy of your progress. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings. Eligible non-consumable purchases can be restored through the App Store; this does not restore spent consumable currency or all game progress.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated when the app's data practices change. Contact Voidseal Games at shadowseals.support@gmail.com for privacy or support requests.
